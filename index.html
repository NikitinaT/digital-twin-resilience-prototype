University Resilience under Prolonged Disruption

Digital Twin Prototype for Situational Awareness and Decision Support
Static demo · GitHub Pages

This prototype shows a disrupted university environment and illustrates how a campus-scale Digital Twin can support leadership awareness and operational decisions when the university must continue functioning during prolonged disruption.

What this prototype shows

▣
University-scale Digital TwinBuildings, infrastructure, environment, and operational state represented as one connected model.
⚠
Disruption environmentElectricity blackout, physical damage, communication loss, access restrictions, and severe weather.
👥
Situational awarenessA shared picture for university leadership, operational teams, researchers, students, and infrastructure services.
▥
Decision supportImpact assessment and prioritisation of actions to maintain critical university functions.
Video: animated scenario visualization of a university operating under disruption. Data are synthetic and used for research demonstration.

Scenario Examples

⚡

S1 — Energy Disruption

Partial or complete power outage affecting campus operations and critical facilities.

Impacts: labs, IT systems, lighting, heating/cooling.

▦

S2 — Physical Damage

Damage to buildings and infrastructure caused by external impact or hazardous events.

Impacts: accessibility, safety, facility availability.

⌁

S3 — Communication Loss

Loss or degradation of internet, mobile connectivity, or internal communication systems.

Impacts: coordination, remote work, data exchange.

🔒

S4 — Access Restriction

Restricted access to parts of the campus due to safety, security, or external conditions.

Impacts: mobility, deliveries, emergency response.

☁

S5 — Extreme Weather

Severe weather conditions affecting infrastructure, safety, and campus continuity.

Impacts: heat, flooding, wind damage, transport disruption.
